# Housing and real estate update

## Find your home

Open **Stuff → Residence, landlord & homes to rent**. Childhood housing is family supported. Adults without a residence receive a supported family-room rental rather than silently becoming homeless. There are 12 rental choices: family/shared rooms, studios, apartments, tiny homes, cottages, townhouses, family homes, penthouses and villas.

Signing a rental lease holds a refundable one-month deposit. Rent is paid annually on Age Up, after salary; unpaid rent becomes visible arrears. Deposits count toward net worth and cannot be claimed twice. Moving refunds the deposit after rent deductions; remaining unpaid rent becomes a debt. Buying a primary home also closes the former rental. Other living expenses still apply separately.

Your rental menu includes your landlord, relationship meter, conversations, repair requests, complaints, paid housing mediation, arrears payments, renewal negotiation and optional contents insurance. Renewals propose a 4% increase; negotiation can retain the old rent or reduce the proposed increase. Your current rate remains until you accept a renewal or move. Repairs, inspections and neighbor complaints appear in the housing history and residence menu.

## Become a landlord

Your occupied primary home cannot also be rented out. Move into another vacant owned property or sign a rental lease first. Open an extra property in **Stuff → Owned → Property → Manage asset**.

1. Schedule its rental listing. The property goes on the market at the **next successful Age Up**, never immediately.
2. Review three candidates: credit, job, annual income, income-to-rent ratio and prior evictions.
3. Accept one candidate to sign a two-year lease. No income is earned for vacant or merely listed properties, or in the signing year.
4. Rent is collected at subsequent Age Ups and shown in the annual budget. Credit and eviction history affect missed-payment risk. Tenant requests, arrears, relationship, lease dates and last income are shown in the property menu.
5. Talk, arrange repairs, recover part of overdue rent through a payment plan, renew the lease or serve notice. Tenant deposits are held separately from spendable cash. Notice takes until the next Age Up, with rent due for that final year; rent deductions from the deposit are recorded once. Unrecovered former-tenant arrears remain in the property record.

At lease expiry there is a one-year renewal/notice period. Renew at the same rate or let the tenant leave. Occupied properties cannot be moved into, sold or gifted until the tenant leaves. Empty listings can be withdrawn. Existing saves with rented extra properties migrate to signed leases with stable tenant identities, preserving their income stream. The primary home remains owner occupied.

## Incidents and protection

Floods, wildfires, earthquakes and hurricanes can affect multiple owned homes and parked cars. You choose repairs/insurance claims or unrepaired condition and value losses. Burglary, street robbery and the existing car-theft and valuables-theft events remain available. Owner home/car insurance lowers eligible repair costs; renter contents cover reduces personal losses. Locks and alarms reduce burglary damage. Incidents wait behind existing game decisions and resolve once. Linked vehicle debt is preserved by the existing loss-settlement system.

These are simplified game rules, including deposits, notice, coverage and licensing; they do not vary by real jurisdiction.

## Real estate special career

Open **Work → Special career → Real estate**. From age 18, complete three licensing courses, limited to one per year. Leave other employment, internships, elected office and executive roles before starting. While active, job applications and internship/political-work handlers block conflicting work, including stale job-confirmation buttons. Studying and passive property ownership remain available.

There is no guaranteed salary. Eight work blocks per year cover prospecting, client briefs, comparable listings, viewings, offers, inspection/financing/appraisal issues and closing. Choose within the client's budget and consider their fit score. Disclose and resolve due-diligence issues before closing. Deals can fall through; old unfinished clients leave after three years. A successful closing pays a 2.5% transaction fee multiplied by your brokerage split, once.

Firm progression:

| Position | Closing / reputation requirements | Commission share | Active client capacity | Annual overhead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Agent | Licensing | 60% | 4 | 600 base units |
| Senior agent | 3 closings, 50 reputation, 3,000 investment | 70% | 5 | 1,200 base units |
| Firm owner | 6 closings, 60 reputation, 6,000 investment | 85% | 6 | 1,800 base units |

Fixed prices follow the game's country economy. Work budgets, clients, earnings and history persist in saves. Commissions and rent appear separately in the ledger; a detailed business-tax system is not included.
